About IndusRoboTix

Pioneering Robotics Solutions in Pakistan Since 2026

Our Story

Founded in 2026 by Furqan Khatti, IndusRoboTix began with a simple vision: to make quality robotics education and components accessible to everyone in Pakistan. What started as a small workshop in Lahore has grown into Pakistan's premier robotics provider.

We recognized a gap in the market - students and hobbyists were struggling with expensive imports, long shipping times, and lack of local technical support. IndusRoboTix was born to solve these challenges.

Today, we've delivered 500+ robotics kits, conducted workshops in 50+ institutions, and helped countless students bring their robotic dreams to life.

Our Mission

To democratize robotics education in Pakistan by providing affordable, high-quality robotics kits and comprehensive support to students, educators, and hobbyists nationwide.

Our Vision

To become the leading robotics ecosystem in South Asia, fostering innovation and preparing the next generation of Pakistani engineers and innovators for the future of technology.

Our Values

Innovation, quality, accessibility, and community. We believe in empowering every Pakistani with the tools and knowledge to excel in robotics and automation.
